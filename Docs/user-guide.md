## Getting Started

When you first open Typer-99, you'll see the home screen with options to create or join a game. You'll need to:

1. Enter your nickname
2. Choose a game mode or join an existing lobby
3. Wait for other players or start the game (if you're the host)

## Game Modes

Typer-99 offers four different game modes:

### 1v1
- A direct competition between two players
- Medium difficulty words
- First player to complete the text wins
- Great for challenging a specific friend

### Free-for-All
- Up to 10 players compete simultaneously
- Medium difficulty words
- Players race to finish the text first
- Great for casual multiplayer typing races

### Battle Royale
- Up to 99 players in an intense competition
- Mixed difficulty words (easy, medium, and hard)
- Players with high WPM and accuracy can send word attacks to others
- Receiving an attack adds more words to your text
- Last player standing wins

### Practice
- Play against AI opponents with adjustable difficulty levels:
  - Easy: ~30 WPM
  - Medium: ~60 WPM
  - Hard: ~90 WPM
  - Expert: ~120+ WPM
- Great for improving your skills before competing against real players

## Creating a Game

To create a new game:

1. Enter your nickname in the field provided
2. Select a game mode from the options
3. For Practice mode, select an AI difficulty level
4. Click the "Create Game" button
5. You'll be taken to the lobby where you can:
   - See the lobby code to share with friends
   - Wait for other players to join (except in Practice mode)
   - Start the game when ready (as the host)

## Joining a Game

To join an existing game:

1. Enter your nickname
2. Enter the lobby code provided by the host in the "Lobby Code" field
3. Click "Join Game"
4. You'll join the lobby and can see other players who've joined
5. Wait for the host to start the game

## Playing the Game

Once the game starts:

1. A countdown will appear (3, 2, 1...)
2. When the countdown ends, the typing text will appear
3. Start typing the displayed text as accurately and quickly as possible
4. You'll see your progress and the progress of other players in real-time
5. In Battle Royale mode, you may receive word attacks which add more text to type
6. The game ends when all players complete the text or when time runs out

## Keyboard Controls

- **Type normally** to enter text
- **Backspace** to delete characters
- **Space** to move to the next word
- **Click the text area** if you need to refocus

The game automatically focuses on the typing area, but you can click it if focus is lost.

## Understanding Your Stats

During and after the game, you'll see several statistics:

- **WPM (Words Per Minute)**: How fast you're typing
- **Accuracy**: Percentage of correct keystrokes
- **Progress**: Visual indicator of how much text you've completed
- **Score**: Points earned based on WPM, accuracy, and placement
- **Rank**: Your final position compared to other players

The final results screen will show detailed statistics for all players including:
- Final WPM
- Accuracy percentage
- Total score
- Mistyped words count

## Customizing Themes

Typer-99 allows you to customize the look and feel of the game:

1. Click the theme icon (sun/moon) in the top-right corner
2. Select from the available themes or create a custom theme
3. To create a custom theme:
   - Click "Create Custom Theme"
   - Name your theme
   - Customize colors for background, text, primary, etc.
   - Click "Save Theme"

Your custom themes will be saved locally and available whenever you play.

## Tips and Strategies

### For Improving WPM
- Focus on accuracy first, speed will follow
- Practice regularly with the Practice mode
- Start with easier difficulty and gradually increase

### For Battle Royale Mode
- Maintain both speed and accuracy to unlock word attacks
- Save attacks for strategic moments
- Watch out for players with high WPM - they may attack you

### For 1v1 Duels
- Consistency is key - avoid making errors that require backspacing
- Stay focused on your own text, not your opponent's progress
